# Luma how-to videos

10 silent 16:9 (1920×1080) screen-recording guides for tryluma.trade. Each one walks
through the real Luma app UI with a cursor, step captions and a closing tryluma.trade card.

| # | Guide |
|---|---|
| 01 | Connect your wallet |
| 02 | Fund your wallet |
| 03 | Link your spending wallet |
| 04 | Set your round-up rules |
| 05 | Authorize round-ups |
| 06 | Choose your stock mix |
| 07 | Explore the stock universe |
| 08 | Make a payment |
| 09 | Auto-invest & portfolio |
| 10 | Activity & privacy |

Recorded against a local copy of the app's own HTML/CSS/JS with its real public protocol
data (health, stock universe, contracts). The wallet, balances, holdings and activity are a
**demo account** (marked on screen); no transaction is ever sent — each flow stops at the
app's review dialog.

Re-record: `SITE=<local copy of tryluma.trade> node src/record.js [n ...]`

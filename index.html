Armor

Six plates. Six ways a memecoin dies.

Every rug needs a function to pull. These are the functions, the selectors the scanner hunts for in bytecode, and how an Ironclad coin is built without them.

Vector · mint0x40c10f19

Hidden mint

The deployer prints a billion fresh tokens and sells them into your bid.

Sealed. Supply is set once in the constructor. No mint function exists.

Vector · tax0x8b4cee08

Sell tax trap

Tax goes from 0% to 99% after launch. You can buy. You can't sell.

Sealed. Zero tax. A transfer moves exactly what you send.

Vector · blacklist0x153b0d1e

Blacklist

Your wallet lands on a list. Your tokens stop moving.

Sealed. The contract keeps no address lists of any kind.

Vector · pause0x8456cb59

Kill switch

One call freezes every transfer while insiders exit elsewhere.

Sealed. No owner, no pause, no admin role. Nobody holds a switch.

Vector · proxyEIP-1967 slot

Proxy swap

The code you audited gets replaced overnight with code that drains you.

Sealed. Not a proxy, no delegatecall. The bytecode is final.

Vector · liquidityLP tokens

Liquidity pull

The deployer withdraws the pool and the chart goes vertical, downward.

Your move. A token contract can't lock a pool it doesn't own. Burn or time-lock your LP tokens and post the transaction. The forge walks you through it.
